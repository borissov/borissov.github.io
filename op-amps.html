Approximate typical values (25°C) from memory of the datasheets — verify against the original datasheet for real designs. Noise is at 1 kHz. Year is the approximate year of introduction, from memory ("—" = not sure; for some parts it is the earliest dated datasheet or product I could find). THD+N is the typical datasheet figure near 1 kHz (test conditions differ between parts, "—" = not known). SNR is calculated, not from datasheets: signal level vs. the op-amp's input voltage noise over the chosen bandwidth (resistor and current noise ignored, so it is a best case), Iq is per channel, Vos is typical.
